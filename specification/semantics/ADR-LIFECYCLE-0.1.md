# ADR Lifecycle Semantics 0.1

## Proposed

The decision is under discussion and is not yet authoritative.

## Accepted

The decision is approved and governs the project until superseded or deprecated.

## Rejected

The proposal was considered and explicitly not adopted.

## Deprecated

The decision remains part of project history but should no longer guide new work.

## Superseded

A newer ADR replaces the decision. The newer ADR should be identified in the document.
